How to use this vault

Note conventions

  • Hubs explain a domain and point to atomic notes.
  • Atomic notes define one concept, its boundary, a mental model, design choices, failure modes, and related links.
  • Concept, pattern, and protocol notes end with a Sources and further reading section. Each link was checked against the live page on the stated date; the source registry aggregates the shared set.
  • Tool notes are dated snapshots. Re-check their official documentation before making adoption decisions.
  • Forecasts are labelled as inference; claims about current behavior have sources in Sources.
  • Security labs use harmless toy objectives and defensive assertions; they are not recipes for bypassing live services.
  • Every note carries a navigation bar at its end — ← Previous · Home · Next → — backed by matching prev:/next: frontmatter. The bar reads the whole sub-vault in reading order (65 notes, section by section, nothing skipped) and is generated by ~/workspace/aieng-audit/add_nav.py (see its CHAIN list). New notes must be added to that list and the script re-run; it re-verifies the full chain before patching and is idempotent (strips old bars/fields and rebuilds).
  • Content notes (concepts, patterns, guides, protocols, security, worksheets, checklists, playbooks) open with The gist — a callout box right under the title, written in plain English for someone with no technical background: what the thing is, why it matters, and what the note will teach. Indexes, hubs, maps, glossaries, and template libraries skip the intro entirely and go straight to their content.

Maintaining notes

  • Update cadence: Tool and framework notes should be re-verified quarterly or when a major version ships. Concept notes are evergreen — update when the field's understanding materially changes.
  • Adding sources: When a note's "Sources and further reading" section grows, add the new entry to Sources in the matching bucket. One bullet per source with the claim it grounds.
  • Staleness flags: Tool notes carry last_verified in frontmatter. If a note is older than 6 months, flag it for re-verification before relying on its claims.
  • Validation: Run the vault validator before committing material changes to catch broken links, missing frontmatter, or duplicate entries.
