I got into jailbreaking for the same reason I get into most technical hobbies: I wanted to understand what the device could do when the defaults were not the limit.

```text
  first jailbreak     tweaks, emulators, curiosity
  later devices       waiting for the right release
  final attempts      mostly aesthetics, less stability
  stock software      sideloading and fewer crashes
```

The experience changed over time. Sideloading replaced some of the old reasons to jailbreak, while the operating system itself picked up more customization. The remaining tweaks were fun, but not useful enough to justify the instability.

I still miss the icon themes and the feeling of making a phone uniquely mine. I do not miss restarts, broken bypasses, or losing an afternoon to a device that would not recover.

It was great while it lasted. For me, the trade stopped making sense.
