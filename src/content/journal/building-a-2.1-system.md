My desk system is a pair of bookshelf speakers, a subwoofer, a compact amplifier, and a streamer that handles the digital side. The whole point was proper stereo with real low end without turning the desk into a rack of boxes.

```text
  streamer → amplifier → bookshelf speakers
                    └──→ subwoofer
```

## What mattered most

The streamer simplified the chain. It handled room correction and the crossover, which meant I could spend time on placement instead of stacking more hardware.

The subwoofer also mattered more than I expected. Crossing over around the low end took pressure away from the bookshelf speakers and made the whole system sound larger.

Room correction helped, but placement came first. Software can reduce a bad room response; it cannot make a badly placed subwoofer disappear.

The next upgrade would be for convenience and inputs, not because the current system needs more power.
