My desk setup is 2.1: KEF Q150s, an SVS SB1000 Pro, a WiiM Ultra, and a Fosi ZA3. Including the sub, it came to about $1000, mostly through a Marketplace deal on the sub and deal hunting on Amazon. I wanted proper stereo with real sub-bass for less than a store-bought 2.1 system would cost.

## The chain

```text
  WiiM Ultra  →  Fosi ZA3  →  KEF Q150s
       │
       └──────────────→  SVS SB-1000 Pro
```

The WiiM Ultra streams, does the room EQ, and handles the sub crossover, with the SB1000 Pro handling everything below it. The Fosi ZA3 is a small class-D amp, and it's more than enough to drive the Q150s. If you want more headroom or balanced inputs, a pair of Fosi V3 monos does the same job in stereo.

## Why the WiiM Ultra changed things

The WiiM replaced what would've been a streamer, a DAC, and a DSP unit, and the app is usable, which is rare. Because the crossover and EQ live in the WiiM, upgrading the amp or speakers later doesn't mean redoing the setup.

The room EQ has been especially useful because the desk is in a corner, which normally means bass problems. The auto-EQ fixed most of it without any manual fiddling.

## Crossover and placement

The SB1000 Pro crosses over around 80 Hz, which takes the bass load off the Q150s and lets them play cleaner in the mids. I tried a few sub positions before it sounded right, and the room EQ handled the rest.

Put the sub where it sounds best from the listening position, then run the EQ. Room EQ can fix a lot, but it can't fix a sub that's in the wrong place entirely.

## What I'd change

I'd still buy the same chain if I did it again. The next upgrade is probably the amp, since the ZA3 is fine but a stereo amp with more inputs would clean up the desk.
